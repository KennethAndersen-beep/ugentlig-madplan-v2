# Ugentlig madplan 2.0 — value-led delivery
Scope: isolated POC only; no main, no deployment, no extra subscriptions, no local PC.
1. Contract and adapter: preserve catalog evidence, recipe ingredients and both instructions; reconcile store lists and costs. Acceptance: synthetic integration tests pass.
2. Complete recipe pool: seven distinct meals, no vegetarian, max one fish, short and detailed instructions, realistic quantities and nutrition/variety review.
3. Household and store lists: recalculate whole packs and costs, distinguish purchase/consumption/remainder; never silently assign unknown price zero.
4. Food waste: prioritize use within documented storage limits; no invented shelf-life guarantees.
Parallel operations: verify CI and actual research; preserve last approved plan on failure, record dated diagnostics. No production changes.
5. UI: retain existing site features; test responsive layout and persisted recipe preference.
6. Images: use licensed or original assets; fallback if missing.
7. Acceptance: actual prices, evidence and validity, weekly unattended execution, no new costs, user approval before production.
Prioritize small vertical slices and reuse. Stop at budget, scope, architecture or production decisions.
